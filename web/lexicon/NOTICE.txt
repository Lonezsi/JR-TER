The word lists in this folder are built by tools/build_lexicon.py from Hunspell
dictionaries, keeping only plain lower-case words. They are what the rhyme tools on the
Words tab search; they never leave the browser that loaded them.

hu.txt, hu-nouns.txt  Magyarispell, the Hungarian Hunspell dictionary (hu-nouns.txt keeps
        the stems it tags as nouns and adjectives).
